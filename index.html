How I work

Every project is different, but the questions I ask stay the same. Four notes pinned above my desk.

01
Understand

Talk to the people, read the data, and find the real problem before anyone opens Figma.

02
Structure

Map flows, edge cases and content so the hard decisions get made early — on paper, not in pixels.

03
Design

High-fidelity work built on reusable systems, tested with real people while it’s still cheap to change.

04
Ship & learn

Pair with engineering through launch, then measure what changed and feed it into the next round.

I’m Victoria, a product designer who creates intuitive, user-centred designs that solve real problems and make products a pleasure to use. I’ve led a design project end to end, worked side by side with developers, and delivered under tight deadlines.

I bring together creativity, empathy and problem-solving, and I do my best work in collaborative teams — turning ideas into elegant, functional products. I’m based in Ogun, Nigeria, with a background in Communication and International Relations.
