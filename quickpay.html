Overview

QuickPay covers the full consumer journey: getting onboarded, funding a balance, paying for everyday services and sending money to people you trust — without the flow ever feeling heavy.

02

Onboarding

A first run that earns trust in three screens.

The intro carousel leads with the single promise — instant transfers — then reassures on security and savings before asking for anything. A persistent Skip respects returning users.

03

Account creation

Sign-up, trimmed to the essentials.

Just the fields needed to open an account, followed by a 4-digit code to confirm the email. Nothing optional gets in the way of the first session.

  • Required only: name, email and password — each marked, with show/hide on secure fields.
  • One-time code: a masked email and a 45-second resend timer set clear expectations.
  • Disabled until valid: primary buttons stay inactive until the form can actually proceed.
04

Home & profile

Everything one glance from the balance.

The dashboard opens on available balance with Transfer and Top Up within reach, a services grid for bills, and rotating promos. Profile keeps the card, account details and settings in one tidy stack.

  • Balance first: a gradient card anchors the screen, with a privacy toggle.
  • Pay for life: internet, electricity, airtime and TV sit one tap away.
  • Self-served profile: card, security, rewards and support, clearly grouped.
05 · Key flow

Sending money

The flagship flow runs on a dark theme to signal “you’re moving real money.” Each step shows only what the next decision needs, and Face ID guards the moment of no return.

  1. 01

    Quick transfer: pick a recent payee or paste an account, then set the amount.

  2. 02

    Confirm: name, amount and date in one read, with a clear payment method.

  3. 03

    Face ID: a biometric check authorises the send before it leaves.

  4. 04

    Successful: confetti, a receipt link and a route straight back home.

“Transfer can’t be undone once sent” — surfaced before, not after, the tap.
